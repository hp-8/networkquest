# Security policy

NetworkQuest handles Google OAuth tokens and Gmail access, so security reports matter.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **don't open a public issue**. Use GitHub's private reporting instead: on this repo, go to **Security → Report a vulnerability**.

Include what you found, steps to reproduce, and the impact. You'll get a reply within a week.

## Scope

In scope: the local server, the hosted mode (sign-in, sessions, allow-list), and the Google OAuth and Gmail handling.

Out of scope: problems in your own Google Cloud or Fly.io setup.
